Alberta Representatives

Senators, Métis Settlement councils, and other bodies that represent Albertans beyond the Legislative Assembly.

Alberta has 6 appointed senators in the Senate of Canada. Senators are appointed by the Governor General on the advice of the Prime Minister and serve until age 75.

Alberta's 8 Métis Settlements are governed by elected Settlement Councils under the Métis Settlements Act. Each settlement elects a Chair and four councillors every four years.
